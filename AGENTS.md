# Repository instructions

This is a Chinese-language, evidence-backed Dota novel research repository.

1. Read README.md and PROJECT.md before continuing. PROJECT.md owns the project overview, current scope and project-wide backlog. Complete current content is maintained by dimension: 06_story/current-setting.md (setting/system), character-writing-cards.md (characters/relationships), current-plot.md (plot/prose adoption), narrative-writing-rules.md (writing/research workflow). Read the dimensions relevant to the task, then source evidence as needed. 00_meta/research-status.md owns research coverage; 09_training/technique-catalog.md owns technique judgments. 00_meta/work-state.md records this execution only. Historical coverage entries and old next-step notes are not instructions. main is the merged baseline; the authorized research branch holds pending changes.
2. Research cutoff and event dates are explicit. Source publication date is not automatically the event date or what the fictional protagonist knew then.
3. Distinguish F facts, Q attributed statements, O direct observations, I inferences, H hypotheses and X fiction. Current fictional values and adoption states belong to the corresponding dimension documents; PROJECT only summarizes and links them. Source cards retain authority only for supported claims. The author decision log is an audit trail, not another independently maintained current plan.
4. Never promote earlier chat claims to evidence. Do not invent match IDs, item timestamps, hero win rates, player psychology or team voice comms.
5. Player and team states are time-bound. A role title does not establish exclusive shot-calling authority.
6. A numbered patch index is not full patch analysis. Hero release, Captain's Mode inclusion and event eligibility are separate.
7. The protagonist is 18 and a first-year university student at TI10, as locked in `06_story/locked-decisions.md` D03/D15. The earlier age-options document is historical; the exact birthday remains unchosen.
8. Track missing data and failed source access. Do not label a full replay watched or dataset exported unless actually done.
9. Public summaries only: no credentials, private leaks, unlicensed full articles or full video mirroring.
10. Publish research on a branch and PR. Do not automatically merge, force-push or overwrite unrelated user changes.
11. Prefer consequential story choices over micromanaging fictional ward positions or dialogue. Research only the selected scene's necessary dependencies; unresolved decisive mechanics remain proposals, not asserted prose. Do not require whole-period research or replay verification for every fictional scene.
12. Before prose work, read PROJECT.md for authorized scope, narrative-writing-rules.md for current methods, current-plot.md for adoption/continuity, the actual preceding draft, and relevant character/period evidence. The old writing-workflow, system baselines, language and innovation discussions are historical explanations, not separate current rules. Do not import later public personas into earlier periods. Read source-policy.md when introducing factual claims or resolving evidence status.
13. Working prose is not an author-approved style benchmark. Follow the current batch's writing phase rather than an older next-step note; keep story drafting, concrete editorial review, and state updates distinct. These writing instructions grant no additional publication or external-action authority.
14. The author requires continuous progress within the authorized scope: after completing a work unit, begin the next available authorized step without waiting for another “继续”. A progress update, completed draft, or pending prose review is not itself a pause gate. Keep unreviewed work provisional and locally reversible; ask only for a consequential story/character/long-term choice, blocking information held by the author, or genuinely new authorization. Present all viable options and tradeoffs with a recommendation. Research and polishing limits end those activities, not all project work. Preserve the author's core-story and final-adoption rights; see 06_story/narrative-writing-rules.md section 10.
15. Update the owning dimension in place, not another dated supplement. PROJECT owns global status and priorities; setting, character, plot/adoption and writing values have one owner each. Research-status owns evidence maturity, and technique-catalog owns technique selection. Update the decision log only for actual author decisions. Preserve source evidence and historical analyses via archive-index; mark obsolete entry claims where readers encounter them. Consolidation does not upgrade adoption, story-patch validity or testing. Work-state records this task's execution, not a duplicate project backlog.
