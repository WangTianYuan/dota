# Repository instructions

This is a Chinese-language, evidence-backed Dota novel research repository.

1. Read README.md and PROJECT.md before continuing. PROJECT.md is the single complete current project plan and phase; do not reconstruct current state by concatenating historical batch reports. For technique work, read the relevant hero in 09_training/technique-catalog.md, then its evidence only as needed. 00_meta/work-state.md records execution and validation; coverage.json is coverage history, and its old next fields are not instructions. main remains the merged baseline; an authorized research branch contains reviewable pending changes.
2. Research cutoff and event dates are explicit. Source publication date is not automatically the event date or what the fictional protagonist knew then.
3. Distinguish F facts, Q attributed statements, O direct observations, I inferences, H hypotheses and X fiction. PROJECT.md consolidates current approved directions and clearly marked working fiction; original creative material and prose remain in 06_story. Evidence cards retain authority only for what their sources support. The decision log records author choices and replacement history; it is not a second independently edited current plan.
4. Never promote earlier chat claims to evidence. Do not invent match IDs, item timestamps, hero win rates, player psychology or team voice comms.
5. Player and team states are time-bound. A role title does not establish exclusive shot-calling authority.
6. A numbered patch index is not full patch analysis. Hero release, Captain's Mode inclusion and event eligibility are separate.
7. The protagonist is 18 and a first-year university student at TI10, as locked in `06_story/locked-decisions.md` D03/D15. The earlier age-options document is historical; the exact birthday remains unchosen.
8. Track missing data and failed source access. Do not label a full replay watched or dataset exported unless actually done.
9. Public summaries only: no credentials, private leaks, unlicensed full articles or full video mirroring.
10. Publish research on a branch and PR. Do not automatically merge, force-push or overwrite unrelated user changes.
11. Prefer consequential story choices over micromanaging fictional ward positions or dialogue. Research only the selected scene's necessary dependencies; unresolved decisive mechanics remain proposals, not asserted prose. Do not require whole-period research or replay verification for every fictional scene.
12. Before drafting or revising prose, read PROJECT.md for the current writing rules and phase, the actual preceding draft when continuing it, and the relevant character's period-specific evidence. Detailed narrative-writing-rules.md, writing-workflow.md and character-writing-cards.md are supporting references, not mandatory historical read-through. Do not use later public personas as an earlier-period default. Read source-policy.md when introducing factual claims or resolving evidence status.
13. Working prose is not an author-approved style benchmark. Follow the current batch's writing phase rather than an older next-step note; keep story drafting, concrete editorial review, and state updates distinct. These writing instructions grant no additional publication or external-action authority.
14. The author requires continuous progress within the authorized scope: after completing a work unit, begin the next available authorized step without waiting for another “继续”. A progress update, completed draft, or pending prose review is not itself a pause gate. Keep unreviewed work provisional and locally reversible; ask only for a consequential story/character/long-term choice, blocking information held by the author, or genuinely new authorization. Present all viable options and tradeoffs with a recommendation. Research and polishing limits end those activities, not all project work. Preserve the author's core-story and final-adoption rights; see 06_story/writing-workflow.md section 3.
15. Maintain current documents by replacing affected sections, not prepending another dated “latest/current” update. Update PROJECT.md for project choices/phase, technique-catalog.md for technique judgments, and the author decision log only for actual author decisions. Keep source evidence and historical analyses addressable through 00_meta/archive-index.md. Archival task instructions do not restart research or prose. Do not upgrade adoption, story-patch validity or testing status merely by consolidating.
